**Website Marketing Calculator** helps coaches and course creators model the economics of a marketing funnel — from ad spend to booked calls to enrolled clients — in two directions: forward from a budget, or backward from an income goal.

**[Open the calculator](https://redrhino-online.github.io/website-marketing-calculator/)** · **[Source on GitHub](https://github.com/redrhino-online/website-marketing-calculator)**

## Highlights

- **Budget mode:** ad budget + cost per lead → projected leads, bookings, calls, clients, revenue, and profit.
- **Income mode:** income goal + profit margin → the required clients, calls, bookings, and leads, unwound backward through the funnel's conversion rates.
- **Five-stage stepper** (Program, Funnel, Projections, Advertising, Metrics) with clickable headers and a first-run training popup; live metrics include ROAS, max cost per lead/booking/call/client, conversion rate, and gross margin.
- **Real-world discreteness:** floor on forward counts, ceiling on backward counts — no fractional leads or clients.
- **No build step:** single-page Vue 2 + Vuetify 2 from CDN, deployed to GitHub Pages by a single workflow.

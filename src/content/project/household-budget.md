**Household Budget** is a budgeting app for households where bills aren't (or shouldn't be) split 50/50 — three roommates on one lease, or a mortgage divided by income rather than evenly. An equitable split proportional to income instead of an equal split.

**[Open Household Budget](https://budget.davemainville.com/)** · **[Source on GitHub](https://github.com/superdave2u/household-budget-tracker)**

## Highlights

- **Per-bill split options:** each bill can be split **evenly** (divided by headcount) or **equitably** (each person's share is their salary ÷ household income × bill amount).
- **People & income shares:** add people with yearly salaries and instantly see each person's percentage of household income.
- **Per-paycheck contributions:** bills map to per-pay amounts based on frequency, and bi-weekly payers are handled correctly — no overfunded months from the occasional third paycheck.
- **Local-first & private:** all data stays in the browser's localStorage; export/import your budget as text with no accounts or server.
- **Built with Vue 3, Vuetify, and Pinia**, deployed to GitHub Pages by GitHub Actions.

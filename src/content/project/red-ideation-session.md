**RED Ideation Session** is the origin of the **RED Method**: the recorded working session that iterated a marketing framework into existence — **R**eveal Your Offer · **E**ngage and Convert · **D**evelop Your Audience — helping companies organize and commercialize their intellectual property so it's easy to understand, easy to consume, easy to sell, and easy to scale. The raw transcript is preserved; the presentation replays the session as a consultation in which both roles are played by the same practitioner.

**[Open the presentation](https://redrhino-online.github.io/red-ideation-session/)** · **[Source on GitHub](https://github.com/redrhino-online/red-ideation-session)**

## Highlights

- **The RED Method's genesis:** three phases (Customer Insight, Conversion Funnel, Traffic Generation) iterated into the R.E.D. acronym, then expanded into stages, modules, transformations, and deliverables.
- **A consultation, replayed:** the deck presents the method as consultant-and-client dialogue — both roles played by the same practitioner, following his own medicine.
- **Complete framework artifacts:** four client archetypes, ten sequenced modules, done-for-you deliverables (visual model, lead magnet, video script, enrollment blueprint, 27-email sequence), and ten before/after KPIs.
- **Transcript preserved, presentation added:** the full session archived under `sources/` while the Reveal.js deck (speaker notes included) makes it presentable — no build step, published to GitHub Pages.
